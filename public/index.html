Supported units and conversion rules
TypeSupported
Weightµg / mcg, mg, g, kg, oz, lb
Metric volumemL, cL, dL, L
Cups and spoonsAU, US customary, metric, US label cup, Japanese and rice cups. Custom source volume per unit.
US and UK volumeFluid ounces, liquid pints, quarts and gallons, kept distinct. Explicit US dry pint and dry quart imports also convert to volume.
CountsEggs, yolks, cloves, slices, pieces, cans, tins, packets, sticks and bunches. Scaled as counts.
Vague amountsPinch, dash, drop and handful stay as written, with no assumed weight. To taste and as needed do not scale.
Other unitsPreserved and scaled as written, never guessed. Heaped / packed qualifiers stay in notes.

US oz is weight, US fl oz is volume. Unknown source sizes stay unresolved. Unqualified US pints and quarts mean liquid measures; dry measures must be explicit. Gas marks and fan-to-conventional temperature adjustments are not direct unit conversions, keep them as source text.

Reference factors: NIST, Australian measures and UK imperial measures. Calculations keep full precision; displayed values are rounded.
